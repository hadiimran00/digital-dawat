## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Beautiful web and video invitations for Pakistani weddings and celebrations, designed by you with a live preview, shared on WhatsApp, with RSVPs you can actually use.
**What it does:** Customers pick an original template, edit names, events, venues, dress codes, Urdu or English text with a live preview, pay a flat price by SadaPay, and get a tap-to-open invitation link (countdown, events, map, add-to-calendar, FAQ, RSVP) plus a downloadable vertical video made in their browser. Hosts see RSVPs, guest counts and can export CSV, and create personalised links per guest ("Dear Sarah & Family", reserved places).
**Product category:** Digital wedding invitations / e-invites (customers likely search "digital wedding card", "online nikkah invitation", "shadi card", "wedding invitation video", "e-card").
**Product type:** Self-serve web product with manual payment review (marketplace of templates, productised service).
**Business model and pricing:** One-time flat price per template, shown upfront, PKR. Currently Rs 999 (Eid, birthday) to Rs 3,999 (full-wedding templates). Includes web invite, video, RSVP, no per-guest fees. Prices are founder-set, **not yet validated with customers (to validate)**.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Not B2B. Consumers: couples, families and event hosts in Pakistan first, then the South Asian diaspora, then international.
**Decision-makers:** The bride, groom, or a family member (often parents or siblings) who organise the function and send invitations over WhatsApp. **(to validate)**
**Primary use case:** Invite a large, multi-function guest list (Mehndi, Nikkah, Baraat, Walima, Dholki) in one polished, shareable link instead of a printed card or a screenshot.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Look premium and personal to family and friends without hiring a designer.
- Tell guests exactly where to be, when, in what dress, and collect who is coming.
- Get a video to post on Instagram or WhatsApp Status.
**Use cases:**
- Full wedding week (up to 6 events), Nikkah only, Mehndi, Walima, engagement, dholki, Eid gathering, birthday, baby shower.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Wedding invitations in Pakistan need to carry several events, two families, Urdu and English, and reach people on WhatsApp, but options are either printed cards, generic Canva images with no RSVP, foreign sites in dollars, or slow done-for-you services.
**Why alternatives fall short (evidence from public sources, not interviews):**
- Western invite sites hide costs: Paperless Post reviewers describe "free" invitations that needed payment (about $12) before sending and large price increases (Trustpilot, ComplaintsBoard).
- Evite is criticised for dated design and ads (TechCrunch, old).
- Canva is a design tool with no built-in RSVP tracking (RSVPify comparison).
- One reviewer complained a digital RSVP form asked for first names only (Reddit, WithJoy).
- Competitor sites studied (iStudio, Webgency, Naqsh, Ceremonia) are mostly done-for-you: pay first, wait days, conflicting delivery times, hidden add-on prices, payment before you see details.
**What it costs them:** Time, design cost, awkward last-minute RSVP counting, an invitation that looks generic. **(to validate)**
**Emotional tension:** Fear of looking cheap or getting details wrong in front of extended family; stress of tracking who is coming. **(to validate)**

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Zareqia, Ceremonia Invites (Pakistan-focused interactive invites), iStudio, Webgency, Naqsh Digital (done-for-you digital invites) — fall short on: wait time, opaque or add-on pricing, pay-before-preview, no self-serve editing with live preview (varies; only text of their pages was reviewed).
**Secondary:** Canva and Evite/Paperless Post — fall short on: RSVP and multi-event wedding structure, hidden fees, USD pricing, Pakistani events and Urdu layout.
**Indirect:** Printed cards, a WhatsApp image or PDF, a family group chat for RSVPs — fall short on: cost per card, no tracking, no maps/calendar, hard to update.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators (all true in the product today):**
- Flat PKR prices shown before editing; you see the finished design before paying.
- Self-serve live-preview editor; 23 original templates, two built on public-domain museum artwork.
- Built for Pakistani weddings: up to 6 events, Bismillah option, family names, per-event dress code, Urdu right-to-left.
- Tap-to-open invite with countdown, maps, add-to-calendar, FAQ; RSVP with guest counts and CSV export.
- Personalised guest links (name greeting, reserved places).
- Video invite rendered in the customer's own browser in four styles.
- Pay with SadaPay and upload a receipt; invitation goes live after confirmation.
**How we do it differently:** Productised self-serve instead of a designer queue.
**Why that's better:** Faster, cheaper, transparent. **(to validate)**

## Objections
| Objection | Response (draft, to validate) |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| "Is it safe to pay by transfer to an unknown shop?" | Show who is behind it, price upfront, confirmation within a stated time; refunds if not published (policy still a draft). |
| "Will my guests (older relatives) be able to open it?" | One WhatsApp link, no app or login; opens in any phone browser. |
| "Will the video work on my phone?" | Tested on desktop Chrome only so far; **verify on iPhone and Android before claiming**. |
**Anti-persona:** Customers who want a bespoke, hand-designed card, or print-ready physical cards.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Printed cards cost and take time; Canva images can't track RSVPs; foreign sites charge in dollars and hide fees.
**Pull:** Looks premium, one link, RSVP tracking, Pakistani events, flat PKR price.
**Habit:** Sending a picture on WhatsApp and counting replies by hand.
**Anxiety:** "Will older relatives understand it?", "Can I trust this site and a bank/wallet transfer?", "What if I need to change a detail?"
